How it works

From intake to ongoing care.

Every GPLX program follows the same shape: intake, labs, diagnosis review, then treatment if the evaluation supports it.

The process

Four steps, in this order.

Not every visitor qualifies for treatment. We tell you clearly when a program is not appropriate.

  1. Structured intake

    A licensed clinician takes your history in detail. What brought you here, what has been tried, and what has not.

  2. Laboratory testing

    CLIA-certified partner labs. Panels are sized to your history — not maximalist.

  3. Diagnosis review

    Your clinician walks through the results and explains what indicates treatment and what does not.

  4. Program design

    If treatment is indicated, we design a program with monitoring cadence, stop criteria, and follow-up visits.

Common questions

How-it-works FAQ.

Do I qualify for treatment?
Not every visitor qualifies. A licensed clinician reviews your medical history and laboratory results and will tell you clearly when a program is not appropriate.
How is pricing structured?
Membership pricing is published on the site. Laboratory and pharmacy costs are billed at cost, without markup.
What states do you serve?
Program availability depends on state licensure and telemedicine rules. Use the state lookup at the top of this page.
How is my health data handled?
Clinical data is kept on HIPAA-capable systems. The marketing site does not accept or transmit PHI.
Can I cancel?
Membership can be cancelled at any time from your patient portal.
Do you guarantee outcomes?
No. Individual response varies and we do not guarantee outcomes.
