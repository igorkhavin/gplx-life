Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide (NAD+) is a vital coenzyme found in every cell of the body. It plays a critical role in energy production, DNA repair, and cellular regeneration. NAD+ levels naturally decline with age, stress, and illness, and replenishing them helps restore optimal mitochondrial function, mental clarity, and overall vitality.
