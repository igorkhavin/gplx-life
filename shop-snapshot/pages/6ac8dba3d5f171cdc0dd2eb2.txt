Selank is a synthetic heptapeptide derived from the naturally occurring immunomodulatory peptide tuftsin. It is engineered to provide potent anxiolytic effects without the sedative or amnesic side effects typically associated with traditional anti-anxiety medications.

This peptide is designed for individuals seeking to manage stress, improve cognitive clarity, and support neurological health by modulating neurotransmitters like dopamine and serotonin while providing neuroprotective benefits.




Benefits

Reduces anxiety and emotional tension without sedation

Enhances cognitive function, focus, and memory retention

Improves mood and mental resilience during high-stress periods

Provides neuroprotective effects by increasing BDNF levels

Supports immune system modulation through tuftsin-derived pathways

Non-habit forming with no potential for physical dependence
