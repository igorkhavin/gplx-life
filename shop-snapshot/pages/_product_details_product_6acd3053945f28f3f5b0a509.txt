Labs, doctor's consult, and your first prescription — one package, zero guesswork.

Low energy, stubborn body fat, poor sleep, flat drive. If that sounds familiar, you don't need another supplement — you need answers. The TRT Initial Package gives you a complete clinical starting point: comprehensive hormone labs, a one-on-one consult with a licensed physician, and, if you qualify, testosterone cypionate shipped to your door. Everything you need to find out where you stand and start treatment the right way.

What's included
Comprehensive hormone panel through Rythm Labs, covering the markers a physician needs to evaluate testosterone therapy safely — not a single number, a full picture.
A dedicated telehealth consult with a licensed doctor who reviews your results with you, answers your questions, and builds a plan around your goals.
Testosterone cypionate, prescribed by your physician and delivered discreetly, if treatment is clinically appropriate for you.
Ongoing access to your patient portal, where your results, consent forms, and appointments live in one place.
How it works
Purchase and onboard. Within minutes you'll receive two short intake forms and two consent forms (one for treatment, one for lab work). Complete them online — it takes about 10 minutes.
Get your labs. Our team places your lab order with Rythm Labs. You'll receive your kit and instructions, complete the draw, and ship it back with the prepaid label.
Review your results. Once your results are in, we upload them to your patient portal and send you the medical intake for testosterone cypionate. A member of our care team will call to walk you through it.
Meet your doctor. Book your consult directly in the portal. Your physician reviews your labs, your history, and your goals, and determines whether TRT is right for you.
Start treatment. If prescribed, your testosterone cypionate ships straight to you, along with clear dosing instructions from your doctor.
Who this is for

Men 18+ who are experiencing symptoms consistent with low testosterone and want a medically supervised, lab-verified approach — not a guess, not a gray-market vial.

Why start with a package instead of a single consult?

Because a consult without labs is a conversation, not a diagnosis. Bundling labs, the doctor's visit, and your first prescription means no separate orders, no waiting between steps, and no surprise costs. You get the full clinical workup and the treatment decision in one streamlined path.
