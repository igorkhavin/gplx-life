Tirzepatide is a once-weekly injectable medication that acts as a dual GIP and GLP-1 receptor agonist. It regulates insulin secretion, slows gastric emptying, and reduces appetite. Clinical studies show that Tirzepatide significantly improves blood sugar control and supports substantial, sustainable weight loss when used under medical supervision. This higher-concentration formulation is intended for patients on advanced maintenance dosing.

Benefits

Promotes significant and sustainable weight loss

Improves blood glucose regulation and insulin sensitivity

Reduces appetite and food cravings

Supports cardiovascular and metabolic health
