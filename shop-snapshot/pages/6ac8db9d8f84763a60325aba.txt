Kisspeptin is a naturally occurring neuropeptide that acts as a key regulator of the reproductive hormone system. It stimulates the hypothalamus to release gonadotropin-releasing hormone (GnRH), which in turn triggers the production of luteinizing hormone (LH) and follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH). Kisspeptin encourages the body to restore its natural hormonal balance rather than introducing external hormones.

Benefits

Supports natural production of testosterone and estrogen

Promotes healthy reproductive function and fertility

Helps regulate the hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal (HPG) axis

May improve energy levels, mood, and libido

Encourages the body to restore its own hormonal rhythm
