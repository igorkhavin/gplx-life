IGF-1 LR3 (Insulin-like Growth Factor 1 Long Arg3) is a modified, long-acting analog of naturally occurring IGF-1. It promotes muscle growth through hyperplasia (increased cell numbers) and enhanced protein synthesis. IGF-1 LR3 also supports fat metabolism, tissue repair, and recovery from intense physical activity.

Benefits

Promotes muscle growth and enhanced protein synthesis

Supports fat metabolism and improved body composition

Accelerates tissue repair and recovery

Enhances nutrient partitioning and energy utilization

Supports overall cellular regeneration and vitality
