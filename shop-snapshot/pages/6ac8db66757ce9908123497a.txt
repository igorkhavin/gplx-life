A complete hormonal, metabolic, and cardiovascular assessment for patients — built to support precise dosing, safety monitoring, and long-term optimization.

Rythm Labs provides an in-depth evaluation of the biomarkers that matter most for patients on or seeking TRT, HRT, GLP-1 support, and basic health values. This panel covers core hormone levels alongside thyroid, cardiovascular, metabolic, hematological, and organ function markers — giving providers a complete picture to support safe, effective, and individualized therapy.

Includes the following tests:

Hormone Balance

Total & Free Testosterone (Calculated) – Measures total testosterone and calculates free testosterone for accurate therapy monitoring.
Sex Hormone-Binding Globulin (SHBG) – Evaluates the protein that binds testosterone, helping determine how much hormone is actually available for the body to use.
Estradiol (E2) – Monitors estrogen levels to help detect hormonal imbalances and guide therapy adjustments.

Thyroid Function

TSH (Thyroid-Stimulating Hormone) – Assesses thyroid signaling, which can influence overall hormonal balance and energy.
Free T3 – Measures active thyroid hormone driving metabolism and energy levels.

Cardiovascular Health

ApoB – A leading predictive marker for cardiovascular risk.
Total, LDL & HDL Cholesterol – Full lipid profile to monitor cardiovascular health.
LDL-C / ApoB Ratio – Assesses cholesterol particle size for a deeper view of cardiovascular risk.
hsCRP – Detects low-grade systemic inflammation not captured by a standard lipid panel.
Remnant Cholesterol & Total Cholesterol/HDL Ratio – Additional markers for cardiovascular risk stratification.

Metabolic Health

Fructosamine – Tracks average blood sugar control over recent weeks.
Triglycerides & Triglycerides/HDL Ratio – Evaluates fat metabolism and early signs of insulin resistance.
Vitamin D – Supports hormone production, immune function, and mood.
Ferritin – Monitors iron storage, which affects energy levels and red blood cell production.
Uric Acid – Reflects metabolic and cellular health.
Albumin – Indicates nutritional and protein status.

Blood Health

Hemoglobin & Hematocrit – Monitors red blood cell count and blood thickness.

Kidney & Liver Function

Creatinine – Evaluates kidney filtration and function.
ALP (Alkaline Phosphatase) – Assesses liver and bone health.
GGT – Sensitive marker for early changes in liver health.
