Oxytocin Acetate is a synthetic form of oxytocin, a naturally occurring neuropeptide hormone involved in social bonding, stress regulation, and emotional well-being. It supports mood stability, may help reduce anxiety and cortisol levels, and plays a role in cardiovascular health and tissue repair.

May benefit cardiovascular health and metabolic regulation
