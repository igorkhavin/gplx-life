Semax is a synthetic nootropic peptide derived from a fragment of adrenocorticotropic hormone (ACTH 4-10). It enhances cognitive performance including memory, focus, and learning capacity while providing neuroprotective benefits. Semax increases Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor (BDNF) and modulates dopamine and serotonin levels to support overall brain health.

Benefits

Enhances memory, focus, and learning capacity

Increases BDNF for improved neuroplasticity

Supports mood stability through dopamine and serotonin modulation

Provides neuroprotective and antioxidant benefits

May support cognitive resilience and recovery from brain injury
