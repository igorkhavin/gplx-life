This product requires a doctor’s consultation. Each pack contains 30 capsules.

Anastrozole: An aromatase inhibitor used to maintain optimal hormone balance by preventing excess estrogen conversion. It helps reduce estrogen-related side effects such as water retention and mood fluctuations, supporting a more stable response to hormone therapy.

Benefits:

Controls estrogen conversion during therapy

Reduces water retention and bloating

Supports stable mood and energy

Balanced hormones for overall well-being
