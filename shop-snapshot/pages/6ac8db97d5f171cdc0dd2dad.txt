TB 500 is a synthetic peptide derived from Thymosin Beta-4, a naturally occurring protein involved in tissue repair, regeneration, and cellular migration. It is widely used to support healing, reduce inflammation, and enhance recovery from injuries.

Benefits

Accelerates healing of muscles, tendons, ligaments, and connective tissues

Reduces inflammation and supports tissue repair

Promotes cellular migration and regeneration

May improve flexibility and joint health

Supports recovery after injury, surgery, or intense physical activity
