This product is lyophilized and requires reconstitution. It comes with a home kit.

Melanotan 2 is a synthetic analogue of the alpha-melanocyte-stimulating hormone ($\alpha$-MSH). It works by non-selectively activating melanocortin receptors (MC1, MC3, MC4, and MC5), which are responsible for skin pigmentation, appetite regulation, and sexual function.

This peptide is primarily used by individuals looking to achieve a deep skin tan without extensive UV exposure, while also leveraging its secondary effects on metabolic rate and libido enhancement.




Benefits




Stimulates rapid skin tanning by increasing melanin production

Suppresses appetite, aiding in weight management and fat loss

Enhances libido and sexual function (increases arousal and erectile response)

Provides photoprotective qualities against sunburn (though it does not replace sunscreen)

Improves overall body composition through metabolic modulation
