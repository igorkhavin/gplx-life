About GPLX

Physician-directed medicine, built for people who take their health seriously.

GPLX exists because most hormone and longevity clinics are built for volume, not for you. We built the opposite: physician-led, data-driven, and priced flat so you know exactly what you are paying for.

Our story

Started because the alternatives were not good enough.

GPLX was founded on a simple observation: the state of hormone and longevity care in the United States is not what it should be. Primary care physicians rarely have time to review the full picture. Direct-to-consumer clinics chase a single lab value and sell a subscription. Boutique practices charge for concierge access without changing the underlying medicine.

We looked at what was missing and built for it. Every GPLX member gets a full biomarker panel at intake — 60+ markers spanning hormones, metabolic, cardiovascular, kidney, and safety systems. A board-certified physician reviews the whole picture, not just the flags, and designs a protocol tuned to the member's biology and goals.

Membership is a flat monthly fee. There are no product commissions and no hidden upsells. Medications, when indicated, are prescribed at pharmacy cost. If a protocol is not appropriate, the physician says so and does not prescribe it.

Our members are people who read their own labs, ask hard questions, and expect a physician to keep up. GPLX was built for that conversation.

What we stand for

Four principles, no exceptions.

Physician-Led

Every protocol is designed and supervised by a board-certified physician. No algorithms, no chatbot triage, no autopilot.

Transparent

Flat pricing, no product commissions, no hidden fees. Your labs and records are yours to keep.

Evidence-First

We do not chase trends. Every recommendation is grounded in your biomarkers, your history, and current clinical evidence.

Long-Term

We are not here to sell you a package. We are here to support you for the long term — through quarterly labs and ongoing physician review.

Leadership

Physician-directed by a board-certified medical team.

GPLX is directed by a board-certified medical team with training across internal medicine, endocrinology, and preventive medicine. Our physicians hold active state licenses in every jurisdiction where GPLX operates, and clinical protocols are reviewed against current U.S. specialty society guidance.

All clinical decisions — from lab interpretation to medication selection — are made by a licensed physician. Care coordinators support scheduling and messaging but do not make clinical decisions.

Once your consultation is scheduled, you will see your treating physician's name, credentials, and state license on your patient portal.
