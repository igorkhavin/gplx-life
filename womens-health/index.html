Program FAQ

Straight answers.

Do you prescribe bioidentical hormones?+
When appropriate, yes. Bioidentical estradiol, progesterone, and testosterone are prescribed alongside FDA-approved products. The choice depends on your labs, symptoms, and goals — decided by your physician, not by inventory.
Is this only for menopause?+
No. We treat perimenopause (often years before menopause), menopause, and post-menopause. Also women with symptomatic hormone imbalances at any age when supported by labs and history.
What about my thyroid?+
Full thyroid panel is included at intake — TSH, Free T3, Free T4, and TPO antibodies. Thyroid dysfunction is often the missed diagnosis behind perimenopause-like symptoms.
Can I stay on my current HRT?+
Yes. Bring your current labs and prescription. Your GPLX physician will review, continue with proper monitoring if appropriate, or explain any recommended changes.
What about weight gain during perimenopause?+
Hormone shifts, insulin resistance, and thyroid changes all play a role. We look at all three. If weight management medications are indicated, they can be added to your protocol — physician-led, never automatic.
Are labs and medications extra?+
Labs are billed by the lab (Quest or LabCorp) and are typically covered by insurance. Medications are billed by the dispensing pharmacy at transparent pricing.
