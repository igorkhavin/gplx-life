Program FAQ

Straight answers.

Is this just supplements?+
No. We are not a supplement store. Interventions are evidence-based and physician-led. When indicated, that may include lifestyle guidance, targeted supplementation, or pharmacotherapy (statins, GLP-1s, metformin, HRT). No product commissions, ever.
Do you do genetic testing?+
Not at this time. Current evidence supports biomarker-driven decisions over broad genetic screening for most patients. Your physician will discuss when a specific genetic test would meaningfully change management.
What about NAD, peptides, and other longevity therapies?+
Some peptides and interventions are supported by evidence for specific indications and are prescribed when appropriate. Others are marketing. Your physician will explain the evidence for each and never recommend something for revenue.
Do you cover cardiovascular imaging?+
We coordinate with imaging when indicated — coronary calcium scoring, carotid ultrasound, etc. Imaging is ordered and billed separately by the imaging center.
What if I am healthy already?+
Then the value is trend tracking. Advanced biomarkers, physician review, and a plan that catches upstream risks early — before they become downstream disease.
Are labs and medications extra?+
Yes. Labs are billed by the lab (Quest or LabCorp) and typically covered by insurance. Medications when prescribed are billed by the dispensing pharmacy at transparent pricing.
