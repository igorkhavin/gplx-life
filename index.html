Frequently Asked

Straight answers

Is this covered by insurance?+
No. GPLX operates independent of insurance so we can spend our time on care, not billing codes. Your labs may still be reimbursable through your insurance if you submit them yourself.
How is this different from a TRT clinic?+
A traditional TRT clinic focuses on one hormone. GPLX reviews 60+ biomarkers across cardiovascular, metabolic, endocrine, inflammatory, and micronutrient systems, then designs a protocol across the full picture.
What does membership include?+
Access to physician-led consultations, protocol design, quarterly lab reviews, secure messaging with your care team, and prescriptions when indicated by your physician. Consultation visits, medication and lab costs are billed separately.
Is this a subscription?+
Membership is month-to-month at a single flat rate of $39.99/month. One tier, no upsell path, cancel anytime. Each consultation visit is $30, and $30 shipping & handling applies per fulfillment. Labs and medications are billed separately, and consultation and medication charges are processed only after physician approval.
Is this available in my state?+
GPLX serves patients in all 50 states. Availability of specific treatments can vary by state, and your clinician will confirm what is available to you during your intake.
