Important information

  • Care is provided by independent licensed medical providers and is subject to provider availability and to the laws and regulations of the state where you are located at the time of your visit.
  • No prescription is issued without review by a licensed medical provider. Every treatment decision is made by your provider after reviewing your health history and, when required, your lab results.
  • Submitting an intake or purchasing a membership does not guarantee that a provider will recommend or prescribe any treatment.
  • Certain medications, lab services, or pharmacy fulfillment options may vary by state. If a service is not available where you are located, our care team will let you know before you are charged for it.
  • You must be physically located in one of the states above at the time of each virtual visit.
  • GPLX services are not for emergencies. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.
