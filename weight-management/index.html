Program FAQ

Straight answers.

Do you prescribe GLP-1s?+
When medically indicated based on your labs, BMI, and history, yes. GLP-1 receptor agonists (semaglutide, tirzepatide) are prescribed by a board-certified physician after full metabolic workup. Not everyone is a candidate — and we tell you if you're not.
What about compounded medications?+
When appropriate and available, compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide may be prescribed through licensed U.S. compounding pharmacies. Your physician will discuss the trade-offs between FDA-approved brand products and compounded options.
How is this different from a weight-loss clinic?+
Full 45+ metabolic biomarker workup, board-certified physician, transparent pricing. No supplement upsells, no product commissions, no hidden fees.
What if I have prior weight-loss surgery?+
Not a disqualifier. Your physician will review your surgical history and design a protocol appropriate for you — often including nutrition monitoring and adjusted medication choices.
Are labs and medications extra?+
Labs are billed by the lab (Quest or LabCorp) and are typically covered by insurance. Medications are billed by the dispensing pharmacy at transparent pricing — no markup, no product commission.
What if I stop the medication?+
Your physician will plan the taper and maintenance strategy with you. Sustainable results require ongoing management — we discuss this upfront, not after you've started.
